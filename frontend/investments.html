How your balances work

Understand the difference between wallet funds, investment-eligible deposits, and earnings.

i

Wallet balance: your recorded account funds.

Investable deposit balance: the remaining approved deposit principal eligible for new investment purchases.

Withdrawable earnings: earnings recorded as eligible for withdrawal, subject to applicable account rules and pending withdrawal reservations.

Important: the figures displayed here are informational. The backend must independently validate every purchase and withdrawal.
