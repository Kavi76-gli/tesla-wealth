Frequently Asked Questions

How can I make a deposit?

Open the Deposit section from the dashboard and follow the available payment instructions.

How can I withdraw my balance?

Open the Withdraw section and enter the required withdrawal information. Make sure your account details are correct.

Where can I see my investments?

You can view your active and completed investments from the My Investments page.

I have a problem with my account.

Contact customer support and provide the relevant account information so our support team can assist you.
