HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

Find helpful information about your account and transactions.

How can I make a deposit?

Open the Deposit section from your dashboard and follow the payment instructions displayed in your account. Always verify the payment details before making a transaction. Keep your payment confirmation for reference.

How can I withdraw my balance?

Visit the Withdraw section, review the applicable withdrawal requirements and submit your request using your correct payment details. You can check the request status in your account.

Where can I see my investments?

Open the My Investments section to review your investment records, active plans and completed investments.

I have a problem with my account.

Contact customer support using one of the available contact methods. Never share your password, OTP, MPIN or private authentication codes.

How can I check transaction status?

Visit your Transactions section to review available transaction records and their current status.
